Photolite

Overview: Photoline is a collaborative timeline application designed to help users document and share life's milestones and events, functioning as a modern, interactive photo album. Developed during my first hackathon as a team of four, the project required rapidly adapting to a new technology stack to deliver a sleek, fully functional, and production-ready application.

Frontend: React, PrimeReact

Backend: Go

Key Features:

  • Interactive Timeline: Users can log 'memories' as chronological nodes containing titles, dates, rich descriptions, and photo galleries. Clicking a node expands the memory for a detailed view.
  • Social Collaboration: Integrated a unique friend-code system, enabling users to tag friends in shared experiences. Shared memories automatically populate across all tagged users' timelines, allowing mutual photo contributions.
  • Privacy Controls: Implemented granular privacy settings so users can maintain personal, hidden photos within a shared memory while keeping the rest of the gallery public to collaborators.

Outcome:Successfully delivered a complete, user-ready application within the strict time constraints of the hackathon, demonstrating strong teamwork, adaptability, and technical proficiency in both frontend and backend development.

Evogen

Overview:Evogen was a Unity-based simulation project developed for my A-level coursework inspired by genetic algorithms and natural evolution, with a purpose of being educational. It involved mimicking biological processes. It involved creating a tiled work with food, water and obstacles. Rabbits would randomly spawn onto the map and have to survive, adapt and reproduce. Each rabbit had unique traits that influenced its survival chances, overtime more favourable traits would be passed down due to Survival of the Fitness. The gene pool would reduce and the rabbit population would oscillate around a stable equilibrium.

Language:Unity C#

Key Features:

  • Genetic Algorithms: Implemented a genetic algorithm to simulate natural selection and evolution.
  • Trait Inheritance: Each rabbit had unique traits that influenced its survival chances, and over time, more favorable traits were passed down to subsequent generations.
  • Procedural Generation of Environment: Used Perlin noise to procedurally generate the environment for land and water. Additionally, food and trees would placed randomly on the map. This made the simulation more dynamic and showed how terrain could affect survival.

Outcome:Successfully completed the project within the deadline and achieved full-marks. Future aim of adding other predators such as foxes into the ecosystem to simulate a predator-prey relationship.
